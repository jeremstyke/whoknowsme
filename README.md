## How it works

1. Answer 10 fun questions about yourself (Classic, 😂 Funny, ❤️ Love, 🏫 School, 🌍 Society, 👯 Best friends)
2. Share your quiz on WhatsApp, Messenger, Telegram or by SMS
3. Your friends guess your answers, and the ranking shows who knows you best

Every day, a **question of the day**: answer it, then guess what your friends answered. Keep your 🔥 streak!

Free, in French and English. Premium options (your own questions, each friend's answers) are available in the Telegram version.
